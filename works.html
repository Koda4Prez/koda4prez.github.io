My Work

Throughout my academic career, I've worked on various projects big and small. My favorite, by far, was getting the opportunity to work with Amtrak to accommodate guests' navigation. My approach was to redesign many of the signs seen around the station, such as guidance pylons and overhead signage. Before this project, I had always made things to accommodate myself, but this experience got me much more interested in the idea of designing for someone else's needs.

Besides that, I've spent a lot of time working closely with my colleagues. Currently, I am in the process of internship searching.
