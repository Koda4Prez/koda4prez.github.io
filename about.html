Who Am I?

Buenas! I'm Fred Ayala, a multidisciplinary creator—an aspiring web developer, graphic designer, and photography enthusiast—raised in the heart of the Pacific Northwest. These days, a student is pursuing a bachelor's degree in computer science from the University of Washington with an ambition to become a ux designer. I've even got a coding competition certificate!

The primary programming languages I have studied thus far include Java, Python, HTML, and CSS. Apart from that, I've spent a lot of time using software like Photoshop and Affinity in my spare time to create various graphic design projects. You can also check out my Resume for more details.

To be honest, I am indecisive to a fault. More often than not, I spend too much time on small details, but in the age of creative apathy, I don't see this as a negative trait to have. I would argue that the best work comes from this type of dedication. This website is living proof of that (or so I hope). Much of what drives me to design comes from this artistic spirit. I view UX design and web development as an opportunity to both create something eye-catching and intuitive. I've been working on this site for a while now, and every iteration it gets closer to my ideal website.
